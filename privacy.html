Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Harbisco Trader ("the app", "we") collects and how it is used. The app is a free practice trading game that uses fake money only.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up with email and password or with Google, our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication) stores your email address, your name and profile photo if you use Google sign-in, and a unique user ID.
  • Game progress. Your cash, holdings, XP, level and badges are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not receive this data on a server.
  • Usage and device data. Our hosting and our service providers may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use information

  • To let you sign in and use the app.
  • To keep your game progress separate for each account on a device.
  • To keep the app secure, fix problems and improve it.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we will ask for your consent before using cookies for personalized ads.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase for sign-in. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the website.
  • Google AdSense for advertising, if enabled.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Your choices

  • You can clear your game progress at any time using the reset button or by clearing your browser data.
  • You can ask us to delete your account by emailing us from the address you signed up with.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work if you do.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
