About Harbisco Trader

Harbisco Trader is a free practice game for learning how buying and selling works. Everything in it is simulated, and no real money is ever used.

What you can do

  • Start with $10,000 of pretend cash.
  • Buy and sell six made-up assets whose prices move every few seconds.
  • Watch a live price chart for each asset.
  • Earn XP, level up, and unlock achievement badges.
  • Reset your account any time and try a new approach.

Who it is for

Beginners who are curious about trading and want to understand terms like buying, selling, profit, loss and risk before ever dealing with real money. It is also for anyone who just enjoys a simple strategy game.

Important notice

Harbisco Trader is for practice and entertainment only. The assets and prices are fictional and random. Results in the game do not predict real market results. Nothing here is financial advice, and you cannot win or withdraw real money.

Who made it

Harbisco Trader was created by Harbisco as a learning project. Questions or feedback are welcome on the Contact page.
